# Design System: Syndra

> **`ui/src/app/globals.css` is the source of truth.** This file is a map of it for impeccable, regenerated from the shipped stylesheet and `ui/src/components/ui` after a build merges, never ahead of one. A token here that differs from `globals.css` is stale; a design that is settled but not yet built is not here. This copy was read from commit `ed3776fb` on 21 Nov 2026.
>
> The rules are not copied here. They live in [RULES.md](design_handoff_syndra/reconcile-2026-10/RULES.md) (the standing rules), [FINAL.md](design_handoff_syndra/reconcile-2026-10/FINAL.md) (every settled choice, and motion in §4), [CRAFT.md](design_handoff_syndra/craft/CRAFT.md) (the craft, and its §12 checklist) and the grammar of light in [A-25 §2](design_handoff_syndra/reconcile-2026-10/from-design/A-25-syndra-alive.md). Where this file and one of them disagree, they win. Who Syndra is for is [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md).

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The keeper of a lit door"**

Syndra is the keeper of a lit door (A-25 §1). The sign-in is an arch of violet light, the mark is a lamp, and the two themes are two rooms: a dark room, which is home, and daylight. Both rooms share one geometry, one spacing and one set of parts. Violet carries both, and only its weight changes with the ground.

It is a calm, data-dense console. Most of the interface is neutral. Space, rhythm and type do the work, so a box means something is wrong. Everything has a seat, and structure never moves in response to data: a count that goes from 0 to 12 reorders nothing.

Light is her tone of voice. It moves once when she is warm, rests when she is plain, and is absent when she is grave: on a consent, a refusal, a failure, or anything that takes access away.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two rooms, one geometry. Both themes are authored in full.
- Five colour roles, and colour is semantic, never decorative.
- Space instead of boxes. A hairline, a ground or a gap before a border.
- Everything in its seat. A seat's width belongs to the table, not the row.
- Steady under the hand. A clicked control never moves.
- Small, meaningful movement. Nothing in the page travels more than 10px, and hover is colour only.

## Colors

Near-black with a green cast in the dark room, white cards on a lilac ground in daylight, and one violet across both.

### Primary
- **Violet** (`accent`, `accent-dense`, `accent-text`, `accent-ink`, `accent-soft`, `accent-line`): the primary action and the current selection, one per screen region. It never means "good" or "safe". The fill you press is `accent-dense`, because a 13px label on `accent` falls under 4.5:1 in the dark room; `accent` itself fills only the round selection seat and the accent dot. `accent-text` is the tone for links and the current place. `focus-ring` is the quiet head of the same ramp in the dark room and the text tone in daylight, so the ring is findable against a violet button.

### Secondary
- **Lime** (`healthy`): "nothing needed here". The provider answered, the queue is empty, the read is fresh. One token on purpose, with no soft, ink or line sibling: a dot, a word or a hairline, never a button and never a fill behind text. Daylight takes the deep lime, because the bright one vanishes on paper.

### Tertiary
- **Amber** (`warn` and its family): a deadline or a broken assumption. Expiring access, changes waiting, a system that isn't answering. Nothing is lost yet.
- **Red** (`danger` and its family): takes access away, or reports that something already failed. A solid red fill appears only on the confirming button inside a dialog.

### Neutral
- **The grounds** (`ground`, `canvas`, `surface-0`, `surface-1`, `surface-2`, `rail`): the page, the content column, and the steps a card, a dialog and the rail sit on. Four steps of near-black in the dark room. In daylight every surface is white and only the ground and canvas are lilac.
- **The lines** (`line`, `line-strong`, `divider`): a card's hairline, a control's edge, and the lighter rule between rows that belong together.
- **The ink** (`ink`, `muted`, `faint`, `label`): quiet text is two steps. `muted` is for sentences; `faint` is for times, ends and captions. `label` is `faint`, told apart by its case and tracking.
- **The tints** (`tint-1` to `tint-3`, `hover`): secondary pills, hover and pressed grounds, skeletons.
- **`scrim`** washes the page behind a dialog; **`chart-hatch`** draws a day nobody could observe, never as a zero.

### Named Rules
**The Violet and Lime Rule.** Violet is what you can act on; lime is what needs nothing from you. A screen where the two are confused cannot tell you where to look.

**The Two Exceptions Rule.** Colour for atmosphere is allowed in two bounded places only. The first is the light from the door (`hour-light`, `lit-card`, `rim`, the lamp). The second is Go to's kind colours (A-26; owner, 21 Nov 2026): People, Pages and Roles take the accent turned +44, −46 and −100, worn only while a scope is on, by the pill, the lamp and the light behind the pane, never a state colour and never violet. Their bounds are in RULES ("Colour is semantic"), A-25 §2 and A-26 §0. Nothing else may add colour for atmosphere.

**The No Alpha Rule.** To step text back, use `muted` or `faint`, or recede its ground. Never an opacity on text (RULES G13).

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bricolage Grotesque (with sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Figtree (with system-ui, sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (with ui-monospace, monospace)

**Character:** Bricolage Grotesque is the display face, for the few loud lines. Figtree is the body. Anything an operator might paste into a terminal or a config file is JetBrains Mono.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (500, 34px, 1.1): the greeting, the one loud moment. 28px on a phone.
- **Page** (500, 28px, 1.15): a page's title. 26px on a phone.
- **Heading** (500, 22px, 1.2): a region. A dialog's title is the same step at 600.
- **Title** (600, 17px, 1.3): a card, an empty state, a Notice, an outcome. 19px on a phone.
- **Figure** (600, 24px): a stat. Tabular.
- **Body** (14px, 1.5): sentences and a row's first line, within the 60ch measure.
- **Small** (13px, 1.45): second lines, notes, and every control's label.
- **Fine** (12.5px, 1.4): labels, captions and times. The floor.
- **Label** (600, 12.5px, 0.1em, uppercase): a column heading or a micro-label. The rail's group heading is the same at 0.13em.

### Named Rules
**The Eight Steps Rule.** The scale is these eight sizes, each about 1.27 times the one below. Tailwind's own text steps are cleared, so there is no ninth. The steps do not grow with the window. (`globals.css` marks the scale "recommended, pending owner".)

**The Floor Rule.** Nothing is set under 12.5px.

**The Mono Rule.** A role key, a grant id, a claim name or a token payload is always mono.

**The Tabular Rule.** Every figure (a count, a date, a time, a size) is tabular, so a column holds and a changing number never shifts its neighbours.

## Layout

Three devices, named for what is in someone's hands: phone below 720px, tablet from 720px, desktop from 1080px. Phone is the unprefixed base.

- **The shell.** A 252px rail from tablet up, and a tab bar under the content on a phone. The top bar is 56px, 66px on a phone. The rail has a ground of its own; the top bar and the scroller have none, so the room's light passes behind them.
- **The page.** Content is capped at 1280px and centred between the rail and the window's edge, with at least 26px of gutter. The top bar's row takes the same frame, so the breadcrumb stays over the title. Sentences keep to 60ch and a field to 400px.
- **Rhythm.** 28px under a page header, 40px between regions, 12px from a region's heading to its first card, 16px between cards. A card's one inset is 20px. A row with a button is 50px, a row of text 41px.
- **Seats.** A row is a name, a description, then numbers and actions in seats of fixed minimum width: 170px for a setting's label, 110px for a number or date (right-aligned), 240px for a status, 200px for one or two verbs. An empty seat keeps its place.
- **Phone.** A phone keeps its own, roomier numbers (rows 14px, regions 32px), and nothing a thumb must hit is under 44px. Controls return to 30px and 36px from desktop.
- **Layers.** Four, named for what sits on them: bar, pill, dialog, popover. A component names the layer, never a number.

## Elevation & Depth

The dark room is flat. A card casts nothing: depth is the step from one ground to the next, and a one-pixel hairline. Daylight needs a little more, because every surface is white, so a card there carries a soft violet-black lift with its hairline. Only what floats above the page casts a real shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`shadow-card`: none in the dark room; `0 2px 10px rgba(60, 40, 120, 0.05)` in daylight): cards and panels.
- **Popover** (`shadow-popover`: `0 18px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`; daylight `0 18px 40px rgba(60, 40, 120, 0.14)`): a popover drawn above the page.
- **Dialog** (`shadow-dialog`: `0 22px 50px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55)`; daylight `0 22px 50px rgba(60, 40, 120, 0.18)`): a dialog or sheet, over the scrim.

### Named Rules
**The Recede Rule.** A card drawn from the last read recedes to the page: canvas ground, a stronger edge, no lift, and its dots become rings of their own colour. Stale data is never dimmed.

**The Violet Shadow Rule.** Daylight's shadows and scrim are a violet-black, so the room is never greyed by pure black.

## Shapes

Soft and round. Corners step down as things nest: a card 20px, a panel or a Notice 18px, a block 16px, a field or a choice 14px, a rail item 12px. A dialog is 22px, and 24px at the top as a phone sheet. Every control is a pill, the selection seat is a circle, and a status dot is 6px.

Edges are one-pixel hairlines. A dashed edge means a section that is not open yet, and nothing else (RULES G19). The focus ring is 2px, set 4px off the control so it clears a violet button; it appears at once and fades on leaving.

## Components

Each part is the same part everywhere. The list of every part, with a preview of each, is `ui/ds-bundle/README.md`; the source is `ui/src/components/{ui,shell,names,states}`.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** a pill, 13px semibold. Two sizes: sm (30px) inside a card or row, md (36px) in a page header and a dialog footer. Both are 44px on touch.
- **Accent:** `accent-dense` under `accent-ink`. The one primary act of a region.
- **Outline:** a `line-strong` edge and ink. The default, and what a button on its own is.
- **Ghost:** muted text, no edge. Only the quieter half of a pair, beside a primary.
- **Danger:** an outline in red. The solid red (`dangerConfirm`) appears only on a dialog's confirm.
- **Hover / Press / Focus:** hover is colour only (the `hover` ground, or a brighter fill). A control presses 3%. Accent, outline and ghost carry the lit edge under the hand (`rim`); a destructive or disabled button never does. A disabled button says why under itself.
- **Going is not doing.** A control that only opens a page is an ArrowLink: violet text ending in →.

### Chips
- **Badge:** a small pill at the fine step. Neutral on `tint-2`; accent, warn and danger filled; soft and hollow forms for quieter facts.
- **Chip:** a neutral pill on `tint-2` at the small step.
- **ChosenChip:** a chosen thing with ×, on `accent-soft` with an `accent-line` edge. One part for every picker.
- **A dot with its word** is StatusSeat. Colour never carries the meaning alone.

### Cards / Containers
- **Card:** `surface-1`, a `line` hairline, 20px corners, the card shadow of its room. The header, rows, columns and caption share the 20px inset.
- **Row:** rows are parted by `divider`, lighter than the card's own line. A row never scales and presses to `tint-3`.
- **Notice:** the only tinted box, because a box means something is wrong. Amber for stale or paused, red for failed, neutral for offline. A dot, a title, a sentence, and the next step. It has no violet tone.
- **No boxes inside boxes.** Explanation is a lede or a caption, never a tinted panel.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Field:** transparent, a `line-strong` edge, 14px corners, 15px text. The dense form is 30px at the body step. The caret is violet.
- **Focus:** the edge turns to `accent-line`, with the ring.
- **Segmented:** a pill track on `tint-2` whose chosen segment is `accent-dense`; the fill glides and the labels hold their width.
- **FilterPills:** like Segmented but neutral (`tint-3`), because a filter is not an action.
- **ChoiceRow:** a card you choose. Chosen is `accent-soft` with an `accent-line` edge.
- **The seat:** Checkbox and Radio are one round seat, 16px with a pointer and 24px on touch. Held is ticked grey; can't-be-chosen is an empty faint ring.

### Navigation
- **The rail:** rows at the body step in muted ink, 12px corners. Hover is the `hover` ground. The current place is `accent-soft` with `accent-text`, semibold. Group headings are labels, never links. Counts sit in fixed seats and a zero is hollow.
- **The phone bar:** up to four tabs, each a dot over a fine label; a fifth destination opens a sheet.
- **Structure** is `ui/src/lib/nav.ts` and nowhere else.

### Dialogs
- **Modal:** `surface-2`, a `line-strong` edge, the dialog shadow, over the scrim. Three widths: 420, 520 and 760px. Below tablet it is a sheet from the bottom edge with a pinned footer.
- **Four places for words:** header, body, footer note, footer pair (RULES G21). The dialog ends on its own result, in the sheet.

### Movement
Six movements: tint 120ms, press 180ms, settle 260ms, arrive 420ms, flash 900ms, breathe 2400ms. Nothing on the critical path is slower than 260ms. Light leads shape by 40ms; closing fades first and is quicker. Breathe is the one loop inside the product, beside the lamp's breath. Under reduced motion, travel goes and colour stays. The spec is FINAL §4 and the Motion block in `globals.css`; use the named utilities, never a raw duration.

### The Light
The hour's light behind Home's and My access's heads, the door's crossing once a sign-in, a member's card on its first day, your place in the rail, and the edge under your hand. All of it is governed by A-25 §2: no box ends a light, one light moves at a time, light never holds words back, light is violet, and every light has a still form. Under forced colours it is gone.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour, size, radius and duration from a token in `globals.css`. A component never holds a hex.
- **Do** use the part that exists. One pattern per job: the role picker for choosing roles, Notice for what is wrong, ActionOutcome for what you did.
- **Do** author both rooms, and check text at 4.5:1 on every ground it can sit on.
- **Do** put every action in its seat, and keep the seat when it is empty.
- **Do** keep a clicked control still: reserve the bold width, and put new choices on their own line.
- **Do** draw the still form of anything that moves, and the phone form beside the desktop.
- **Do** end every outcome on a next step, or "Nothing for you to do."

### Don't:
- **Don't** use violet to mean good, or lime on a button or behind text.
- **Don't** fill a button solid red outside a dialog's confirm.
- **Don't** add colour for atmosphere outside the light from the door.
- **Don't** draw a box to explain. `accent-soft` and tint panels never hold explanation, and dashed means not open yet.
- **Don't** dim stale data or step text back with an opacity.
- **Don't** let anything in the page travel more than 10px, scale a row or a card on hover, or loop anything healthy.
- **Don't** use a toast. Nothing times out.
- **Don't** show a raw id as a label, or sample data anywhere.
