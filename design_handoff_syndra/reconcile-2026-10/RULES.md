# Standing rules (the design project's CLAUDE.md, as of 15 Oct 2026)

Every board follows these. Where a board is silent, build to them.


- Elegant, cohesive, human-first experience decides every change. One pattern per job.
- Human-readable names everywhere they can be known deterministically. A page opened from a link already knows the thing's name: show the name while loading and on error, never an id. Raw ids appear only where the id is the point: "Unknown account" (#35), a support reference, Advanced lineage.
- Never show data that has not been read: loading and error frames carry no counts, notes or freshness.
- Degraded shows last known data, labelled with its age, never sample data (N23). With no last read, it shows nothing: the Notice, headers without counts, no rows. Syndra never falls back to sample data anywhere.
- Every dysfunction is one Notice (proposed/Notice.md), in the content column (6a): amber stale/paused, red failed, neutral offline.
- A section that can't be used yet is NotOpenYet (proposed/NotOpenYet.md): dashed line-strong border, a full-strength lead line (neutral dot, what opens it, why, violet link to the step), contents previewed at 0.45 and inert. Stale data is not disabled: it stays readable at 0.62 under its Notice.
- "Paused" is only ever a person's choice (a system draining or read-only). Syndra's own stops say what's happening: "Waiting to retry" (the breaker), "waits until it answers", "stops at that change". A connected system is Offline after 3 missed checks in a row; one or two missed show only to the operator, as a faint line and check pips (lime dot answered, amber ring missed) under a lime Answering (Q-01).
- Vertical rhythm (9b): header → content 28; every list's FilterBar is the same: search left, then Filters (or a single pill set), then Select, right. The read line (freshness, a note, an in-force chip) sits beneath it, 10 below; with no FilterBar above it (a person's page) it belongs to the cards below, so it takes the page's 18 above and 14 to the cards, inset 20 to align with card content; 10 under a search bar; 14 head → card; 16 between cards and notices.
- Tables never truncate what someone decides on (a reason, a role, a date): wrap with text-wrap:pretty. Truncate only identifiers with a full view one tap away.
- Lists that can outgrow a screen (People, Roles, Projects, Requests, Pending changes, Drift) get a search in the FilterBar's search seat. Short lists (Apps, a project's roles, a bundle's roles) get none. Sort lives on the column headings. Name and number columns order; a column of repeated values (a role, a reason, a group, a bundle) gathers equal values together, A to Z, each run under a quiet label with its count, and within it the list's own time order (newest found, soonest ending); free-text columns (a request's why) do not sort; the heading that orders the list by default carries the arrow; where the default is not otherwise settled, most recent on top; no "… first" notes, and never an order Syndra cannot compute (no "riskiest"). Pending changes does not sort: its order is the send order.
- A grouped list (Drift's two directions, a batch in Pending changes, a person's projects) folds: a 28px round chevron before the heading, open by default. Long groups show 10 rows, then "Showing 10 of N" · Show 25 more · Show all N · Back to 10 (returns to the heading). Getting to a section: a page with two or more sections (Drift's directions, a person's projects) puts one Chip per section at the right end of its read line (dot, short name, count, ↓); the read line's left always says how current the list is (ReadFreshness), even when the list is Syndra's own. Once that line scrolls away, the same sections float as a pill at the top centre: the one you are in filled (surface-2), the others pointing ↑ or ↓. A folded section opens on the way.
- Long-list motion is Motion 8a–8d: fold (settle; closing fades first), show more (only new rows arrive), back to 10 (glide to the heading, trim out of sight), re-sort (the list settles in; rows never travel), jump (glide, the landing heading flashes once), floating pill (drops in on settle, leaves on tint). Nothing jumps under the reader.
- Tables: name · description · numbers (8b). Numbers last, 110px right-aligned seat.
- Going somewhere is a violet text link ending in →; doing something is a button. Row actions name their verb (3b), outline sm, no ellipsis, each in a nowrap seat. A card of settings is one row per setting: label (170) · value · way on in the 200px right seat, a link or a named button (Q-03 setup card). When the row is too narrow, the seat wraps and stays right-aligned (margin-left:auto), never under the label.
- Every name that refers to something with its own page (a person, bundle, project, app, role) links there: ink, accent + underline on hover. Not the page's own subject. A cut-off list ("and 11 more") becomes a violet link to the full list, filtered. A role has no page: it links to its project, unless the project is already named beside it (then only the project links). One link per destination per row. Ledes, notices and captions stay plain prose; in-page selectors (a bundle in the Bundles side list) select, they don't link. Causes that aren't things ("Given by hand", "Left the makerspace") stay plain; the person beside them links.
- Anything queued for the operator to send says "Waiting for you to send →" (amber dot, violet link) and opens Pending changes. Never "waiting to be sent": it reads as automatic.
- One write place per setting. A setting is changed in exactly one place; every other screen may show it, read-only, with one line and a link to where it's changed ("Set in Roles that wait →"). No per-row toggles for a makerspace-wide choice.
- A step left is said where you took the first one. Before acting, one line says whether this will finish now or wait. After, the outcome says what happened, and the thing you changed keeps the status "Waiting for you to send →" (amber dot, violet link, proposed/NextStep.md) in its own status seat, with one faint line saying what isn't true yet and why, until the step is taken. No boxes for it. Never send for the operator, never navigate them away.
- Zitadel verdict: dot + word, five words (2b).
- One part per job: choosing roles is always the role picker (route-bundles-v2 2a/3a: one search, chosen chips, folding projects, seat-led rows, locked rows ticked grey with why). Dialogs are Modal + ModalHeader + ModalFooter; the footer note is the before-line. Never a native select or a second list for roles.
- A role that always waits carries the Waits-for-you mark: a half-filled dot after its name, read-only, explained on hover and focus. Where a row has room (a When given column, an editor field, a legend) the words "Waits for you" sit beside it.
- Nav (N18, 3a): Bundles · Automatic rules · Insights at top level; a "Changes" group holds Pending changes, Change history and Roles that wait. The drift sound lives in Account.
- Say "people", never "holders"/"members"/"accounts" for counts.
- Where there are several good options, draw them side by side for the owner to pick; recommend one.
- Only classes present in the DS stylesheet; otherwise inline styles. No hex.
- Operator's name: Kanishk Singh (top bar shows "Kanishk").
- Freshness (86c): every reading has a dot. Colour says whether you can act (lime live and ageing, amber stale and provisional); a ring says it isn't a fresh read from the source, so Refresh won't help.
- Phone (87c, N20): search keeps its seat on every list; below tablet Filters opens as a Modal sm sheet ending in one accent button that names the result ("Show 12 people"). Insights below tablet is the answer in sentences (List view, lens as a Select); the map is for wider screens, and the link keeps the question.
