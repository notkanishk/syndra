Changes in both options
  • "Ready to use" goes. A role that works says nothing more. Only the exceptions speak, in the row's second line: "Not there yet" (amber) after a complete read that lacks it, and "Couldn't check" (grey) when Zitadel didn't answer. Healthy is silence, as everywhere else.
  • The expiry moves onto its role. The separate amber box becomes the row itself: tinted, "Given to you until 20 Oct · 10 days left", and "Ask to keep it longer →" on the row. The role that runs out leads its card. One place for one fact.
  • The summary says it plainly. "Four roles across two rooms. One runs out on 20 Oct." It counts what Zitadel holds and names the date.
  • Recent changes, new. The last 30 days in the member's words, newest first.
