A single action that asks before it cancels

A-21, Q2 (planner decision 9). The sheet plans as soon as it has enough to plan, so it says before the press that giving will cancel a removal that is waiting. The press pushes the existing cancel step at once, filled from the plan, with no round trip; Keep it is first, and the confirm names what goes ahead. A give that cancels nothing stays one step. When the person's access moved while the sheet was open, nothing is written: one Notice says so, and the note under the form is already the fresh one.

1 · Give a role: foretold, then asked

Give Rohan Mehta a role. Member in Laser Lab is chosen; Sam Lee queued its removal two hours ago. The footer note says what giving does today: the date line, the delivery line, then the cancel in one sentence. The button stays Give role. On the press, the form fades and the step settles into the sheet (S2): the removal, who queued it, when, and the reason, with Keep it first and the confirm naming the act. When nothing is cancelled, there is no step at all.

2 · When the plan went stale

Between the plan and the press, Sam's removal was sent. The write is refused, nothing is written, and the sheet says it where the act was taken: a Notice between the header and the form, and the note already refreshed (the cancel sentence has left it). The step never shows, because there is nothing to cancel now. No "stale" and no "plan" on screen: the reader had a sheet, and what moved was a person's access.

3 · Revoke, when the give never reached Zitadel

The planner's delivery is none: revoking only cancels the waiting give, and sends nothing. The dialog says so plainly, and its button is the queue's own verb, "Cancel the waiting give", accent, in place of "Withdraw the queued grant". The result word is Cancelled, and it ends on "Nothing for you to do."
