An app, and what it's told

A-14, revising A-11. Each app has its own shape: the roles it sees and the word it sees for each, the claims it gets, and where they land. A template starts it, the preview proves it before you save, and Debug mode shows one account's real sign-ins as they happen. An app with no shape of its own uses its project's default; a template, or another app's shape, is an offer on top. Each frame is the real shell and components at true width; open one alone with ?frame= and its name.

Carried · settled

From A-11: the five-check trail ending in the token · a draft previews itself with − and + lines · test people and the person form · the save summary that ends on Saved · History and Go back · what the app reads, Matches or Doesn't match · typed fixed values · the refused cross-project check (ADR-002) · degraded, now with A-13 (d): cards from Zitadel's last answer recede · #49 no roles is a full sentence · #90 on a phone the shape comes first.

Gone from A-11 (owner, 3 Nov)

Moving off Actions v1 and its pasted-token comparison · start from a token · the project default and its "What to edit" switch: a shape belongs to one app · the faded line for another app's claim · array, csv and spaced offered as equals · the mixed-format warning on the apps index. The owner removes the v1 scripts himself; apps that exist today are seeded with exactly what v1 sent, so that day changes nothing.

Decisions

1 · The page

Header · What NetBird is told beside the Preview · Debug mode · Test people · What NetBird reads · History. The shape card is one column: the template line, the roles table, the claim rows, where it lands, Save. Basic is that, calm. Advanced only adds ways to make unusual shapes: where it lands as a choice, roles added later, nesting, More claims, text formats, the client id. A saved row shows in both views.

2 · Templates, on top of a working default

An app with no shape of its own uses its project's default: the built-in roles list when nobody set one. The card says what it sends now, then offers Start from a template · Copy another app's shape. Choosing a template unfolds its short form and the preview shows the draft against the default at once. The sheet says what it sends, where it lands, what the app does, what no claim can do, and what to set. Role choices and words don't count as changes since.

3 · Roles for this app

One table: Role · "NetBird calls it" · Included. Every role in the project, A to Z. A rename or a removal is said three times before it lands: a faint "Was …" on its row, an amber before-line naming what NetBird will do ("12 people move to a new group"), and the save summary. What counts as breaking comes from the template.

4 · One row form

Claim · Carries · Remove; a second line only where the kind needs it; every row ends "Sent as …" with the literal. Carries is grouped: from the person, the same for everyone, and (Advanced) More claims. Nesting is its own "Inside" field, so a dot stays a dot. Roles are always sent, [] when empty. Text formats: Advanced only, with a warning (owner, 3 Nov).

5 · Where claims land

Per app: Both | Access token | ID token and userinfo, set by the template, changeable in Advanced. Check 1 becomes "Zitadel's settings for NetBird" and reads the token type and "User Info inside ID Token" where the shape lands. A Bearer token for NetBird turns checks 1 and 5 red, with the fix in Zitadel as a violet link.

6 · Debug mode

Only here, under the preview: one row when off. It captures exactly one account at a time, you by default; there is no "everyone" (owner, 3 Nov). Change account moves the capture and keeps the end time. A live list fills in place, newest on top; arrivals wait for the pointer to leave. One sign-in opens in place: what Zitadel sent, what Syndra answered, the checks, and the claims rebuilt, not the signed token. Signatures never show.

7 · What changes from A-11

History is per app. The save summary's "3 apps on the project" becomes What NetBird will do, and a breaking save needs the I-understand tick (owner, 3 Nov). #50's "nobody named the claim" becomes "Uses Code's default". What the app reads moves below Test people, is filled by the template and says where. The apps index's Format column becomes Claims, with the template on the sub-line.

8 · Copy another app's shape

Any app, this project or another, from the offer or later from the claims part. It lands as a draft with the preview diff and the usual save summary. Claim rows, formats, where they land, fixed values, nested and role-dependent rows copy. Role words copy only for keys this project has; the rest are a faint line, "Not in this project, so not copied". A role-dependent row whose role is missing stops Save until fixed. The two apps aren't linked.

Owner's calls · settled 3 Nov

The owner chose each one on 3 Nov. Where an option was drawn and not chosen, it stays here, quieter, for the record. Every frame below follows the choice.

4 · Text formats for roles
4aBehind Advanced, with a warningOwner, 3 Nov

No app Syndra has a template for reads roles as text. NetBird would make one group called netbird-staff,netbird-admins.

No template chooses text. A shape already sending text shows it in both views, with the warning.

4bRemovedNot chosen

Roles always sent as a list, with no Sent as.

6 · Whose sign-ins Debug mode captures
6One account at a time, you by defaultOwner, 3 Nov

There is no "everyone". Choosing another account while it runs moves the capture to them; the before-line says so and the end time stays.

What the owner ruled out

Capturing everyone signing in to an app. Syndra never holds another person's sign-in unless that one account was chosen, and the filter runs before anything is kept.

7 · A save NetBird or GitLab will act on
7aAn I-understand tickOwner, 3 Nov

Only when the template marks the change as breaking. Save stays off until it's ticked.

7bThe before-line and summary onlyNot chosen

The words said twice, with no tick.

7 · An app with no shape of its own
7It gets its project's defaultOwner, 3 Nov

roles: every role in the project, in Syndra's words, as a list, in both places, when nobody set a default. Templates and copying are an offer on top of a shape that already works.

7cIt gets nothingNot chosen

My first draft. The owner chose a working default instead.
