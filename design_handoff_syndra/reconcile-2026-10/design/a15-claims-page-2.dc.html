2 · The summary

One line per top-level claim, in the token's order: the key in mono, then what it carries in words. Then one faint line: where it lands, and the template. The token beside it is the same lines as literals for one person, so the two read across. Both, never a sentence that paraphrases the token.

3 · The roles table

RoleWordsTable, first and always open. Its word fields are quiet at rest: they read as mono text, show their line on hover and their ring on focus. One click edits. An edited word keeps its border and a faint "Was staff."; a left-out role reads muted. The amber before-line comes with Save.

4 · Claim rows, folded

Advanced only. Closed: a 28px chevron, "Claim rows", and a faint count with the keys ("5 rows · groups · custom_claims holding 4 · both places"). Open: every ClaimRow form as built, Add a claim, then Lands in. Text formats stay on each roles row's second line. It opens by itself only when a row inside stops Save.

5 · Preview and checks

"Preview for [Ada Lovelace]" is the card's title line, with Checked and Check again at its end. Then one verdict line: "All five checks pass", with Show the checks. A failure opens the trail by itself in its compact form, the failing check with its fix. The token always sits under the trail, never inside check 5.

6 · Quiet tools

One card, three settings rows: Sign-ins (the 30-day bars, small, with the count) · Debug mode (StatusSeat "Off" and its one line, Turn on debug mode) · History (the current version in one line; with two or more, Show all N unfolds the table). Each is a deliberate button away from doing anything.

7 · Phone

One column, the shape first (#90). The summary's key sits above its words when they don't fit beside it. Roles stack with the word field full width. Then the preview's verdict and token, then the tools.

8 · Motion

Every fold is Unfold: height settles, contents tint in a beat behind; closing fades first, then shuts. The chevron turns on tint. Nothing travels; the page never scrolls to a fold. The verdict line crossfades in its seat. Reduced motion: each appears and goes at once.
