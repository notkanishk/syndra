The System group, in words

A-20, part three of three. The screens the owner opens when something is wrong were never designed to the rules the rest of the product follows: Zitadel's health says "Not answering" in its seat and "Unreachable" in the Notice beside it; a system's Waiting on you has no empty state; Incoming events shows a JSON dump under "Show details". One word per state, a sentence where there was a code, and one fold, Technical detail, for the machine's words.

What you asked

Go over everything again, including the areas never deeply designed: TrueNAS and storage, Zitadel and the System group, service accounts, Incoming events.

What I recommend here

One word per state: Zitadel uses the connected-system words, Answering, Not answering, Offline, Waiting to retry, Not connected, with the check pips, and a Check again. Nothing is waiting on you is said, in its card, when it is true. Technical detail is the one fold for ids, JSON and commands, for whoever runs the server; everything outside it is a sentence.

1 · Zitadel's health line, one word (P5)

The Zitadel page's health region in three states, stacked so they can be compared. The seat's word and the Notice's title are the same word. One or two missed checks show as pips under a lime Answering and never raise a Notice (Q-01); three in a row is Offline, a neutral Notice, and Syndra keeps checking. Check again is the outline sm beside the read line, as Connected systems has it. The meta never says a count it has not read.

2 · Nothing is waiting on you (P5, P11)

TrueNAS's page, the Waiting on you region, with nothing waiting. Today the region shows its label and nothing under it, and the Unconfirmed changes card exists only when it has rows. Proposed, the region keeps one card, and the card says so with the words A-09 already wrote. The chip in the read line reads "Waiting on you 0", hollow, where it always is.

3 · Incoming events, with Technical detail folded (P11)

One event, opened. Today "Show details" reveals the raw event type and a JSON dump (operations/page.tsx:434-438). Proposed, the row opens by the 28px round chevron into named facts: who, what, where it came from, when, and what Syndra did. Under them, one fold, Technical detail, closed, marked "for whoever runs the Syndra server", holding the raw event. The same part takes the capability ids on a system's page, the mapping's field and value, the Zitadel key's commands and Claims delivery's expected and found values. Drawn inline; proposed/TechnicalDetail.md says what it is.
