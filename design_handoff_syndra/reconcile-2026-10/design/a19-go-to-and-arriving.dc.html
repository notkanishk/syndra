Go to, and the name that arrives with you

A-19, part one of two. Three of the first wave: one way to reach anything by typing its name (P4); the next page painting with the name you clicked already in it (P1); and the promised step landing on the person it was about (P3). Nothing new moves. The continuity is that the thing you pressed is still there when the page changes.

What you asked

Go through the design system and the project in depth and improve on them. Rich, fluid, organic, flowing, elegant. Make the user feel the app is an extension of them.

What I recommend here

Go to: one Modal sm with one Input, opened by / or Cmd K, grouped Pages · People · Projects · Apps · Bundles, A to Z inside each group, nothing pinned. The name arrives: a row seeds the next page, so the header paints on the first frame and the skeleton covers only what the row could not know. Landing on you: "Waiting for you to send" carries the person into Pending changes as a chip.

1 · Go to (P4)

Press / anywhere a field is not focused, or Cmd K (Ctrl K). The sheet rises as every dialog does (S1). "pri" is typed. Groups keep a fixed order and a group with no match is left out; inside a group the names are A to Z by their shown name. Enter opens the first result, the arrows move the selection, Esc closes. Advanced leaves appear only in Advanced. No recent items, no pins.

2 · The name arrives with you (P1)

The first frame of a person's page, opened from the People list. Today the page knows nothing until the read lands. Proposed, the row seeds the page with what it already held: the name, the email, the kind. The header, breadcrumb, avatar and tabs paint at once and hold still; the skeleton covers only the rows, and only after 200ms (S7). Nothing moves between the two screens, which is the whole of the continuity Syndra allows.

3 · Landing on you (P3)

Pending changes, opened from "Waiting for you to send" on Cody's page. The link carried her, so her chip is in force and the read line says what is shown and what Send does. Her name links back to her page (G14). Clearing the chip shows everyone's changes; Send always sends all of them, so the chip never changes what Send does.
