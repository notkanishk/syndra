Motion, alive

The owner asked for motion that is posh, rich, uplifting and subtle, and that makes the product a joy to touch. Code's reading is right: more life inside the rules, not fewer rules. Nothing new travels further. Instead, more of the product answers you, every answer lands on one soft curve, and light does the work that distance would. Each demo below loops; the line under it names the step on screen. Use Tweaks to slow them or see the still forms.

What the four words mean here

Organic

One curve for everything that moves: fast away, long soft landing (--ease-settle). Light leads and shape follows by 40ms, so nothing switches all at once. Groups stagger; nothing marches.

Rich

Depth from layers, not distance. A scrim tints before its sheet rises. A control's indicator glides to your choice. A dot that just went well blooms once. Every surface you touch answers, not only buttons.

Uplifting

Good news gets a gesture: things that went well lift 6px and bloom. Bad news stays still and steady, so it reads as calm, not alarm. Closing is quicker than opening, so the product never makes you wait to leave.

Subtle

10px is the furthest anything in the page travels. 3% is the deepest press. A healthy screen is the stillest thing on it. Counts never roll. Nothing moves under the reader while they read.

Six moments of delight

The places an operator feels something: finishing, choosing, committing, checking. Each is built only from the six movements, travels 6px at most, and has a still form. None loops, and none plays for something the reader didn't do.
