A plan grouped by how each change travels

A-21, Q1 (planner decision 11). The preview groups people, not writes, into three folds in a fixed order: Straight away, Will wait for you to send, Left as they are. The heading says which; the row says what, in the planner's own words. The amber heading is the only colour in the box, so the glance is one dot and one count. One row per person, so nobody appears twice and a person with some of each reads as one sentence.

What you asked

A person sees at a glance what goes now and what waits for them, without losing the per-person reading. Cover a person with some of each, rows that only cancel, refused and No change rows, the apply consent, phone, both themes.

What I recommend here

Group people by whether a step is left for them. Any role that waits puts the person under the amber fold; their row names which role waits and which go now. A cancel-only row sits under Straight away, because it is finished when you apply, and says that nothing is sent. The button carries the cancels: "Cancel 2 and apply to 11 people", the same shape as the cancel step's confirm.
