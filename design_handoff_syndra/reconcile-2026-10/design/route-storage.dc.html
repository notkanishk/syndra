Route · /storage · member

Network storage

A member's own page for their files: whether they have an account, how to sign in, and how to connect. One card per storage system, today only TrueNAS. A three-step line opens every state, so a member can always see where they are: a role gives you access, your account is created, you set a password. A step-by-step connection guide sits one tap away, in a dialog (4b). Each frame is the real shell and components; open one alone with ?frame= and its name.

Carried · as built

Three states, never collapsed: no role, on its way, ready (§20) · the password form is replaced, never disabled, when TrueNAS cannot take it · a withheld share is named with its reason, never silently dropped · no estimates: an age and who is waited on · the pause leads with "your access has not changed" and never says maintenance · a quota of zero is no limit, not a full bar · the password is kept nowhere (§30).

Synthesis · code owed

Each state opens with one display line saying where you are, under the step line · when a password is the next step, its form moves to the top in the violet step panel, the same panel as a system's project step; when it is not, it is a quiet outline at the end · Your shares, You are using and Connecting listed the same shares three times; now one row per share holds its use and both addresses · the usage bar is neutral, amber from 90%: violet is for what you can act on · at most one Notice per card · beside the system's name, a status: green "Online and accepting sign-ins" with when it was checked, amber "Offline since" a time. Amber only when Syndra is sure; it needs reachable_since and checked_at on GET /me/targets.

Owner, 9 Oct · no password while paused or offline

While changes are paused, the button reads Save it for when changes start again and the notice promises the password will be applied later. The code's own comment says the add-on refuses it, and Syndra keeps the password nowhere, so it cannot hold it for later either. Ruled: the form is not offered while paused or offline, first password included. One sentence takes its place and the current password keeps working.
