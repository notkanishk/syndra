Browsing without a second pane

2aProjects fold, as every grouped list doesPicked · owner, 9 Oct

A bundle as a template, or a bundle inside a bundle

Two different acts, each in one place. Start from copies a bundle's roles once, at creation, and the new bundle is yours from then on. Include, in the picker, puts a bundle inside this one as a live part that changes when it changes. Keeping the two verbs in two places means nobody copies when they meant to link, or the reverse.

When the included bundle changes

Safety basics publishes v4, adding Network storage · Member. New members includes it. Does New members change too? This is the one decision the live part needs. Nesting is new: nothing like it ships today, so it needs your yes and a backend change.

2eNew members takes it when you publish New membersPicked · owner, 9 Oct

Why I recommend it. It keeps #43's rule that an edit and its consequence are never the same click, for every bundle. Publishing Safety basics only ever asks about Safety basics' own people. The cost is a step per bundle that includes it.

2fNew members follows along
Rules for an included bundle, in both

One level deep: a bundle that includes others can't itself be included, so there is never a chain to trace. A role that comes both directly and through the included bundle is listed once, with both sources, and stays if either goes. On a person's page the source reads "Via New members, from Safety basics", each name linking to its page. Retiring a bundle that another includes is refused; the dialog names those bundles. Changing an included bundle's roles happens on its own page, linked from its card.

1 · Choosing roles

The built picker is one long list of every role in every project. Both options keep its strengths (one search across every project, roles already in shown ticked rather than hidden) and drop the scroll. Both also let you start from something that already exists: a person who is the kind of member this bundle describes, or another bundle. The same picker is used for New bundle and for Add roles on a bundle.

1aBrowse by project, search across allIts logic carried into 2a

Why I recommend it. The makerspace is organised by room, so browsing by project matches how operators think, and search covers the case where they already know the name. It needs the wide dialog.

1bType to addIts look carried into 2a

2 · The page

The built page puts a side list, the contents, the versions and the people in three columns at once. Both options give the bundle one column and three tabs: what it grants, who has it, and its history. Unpublished changes sit in one violet panel above the tabs, with what dropping a role would cost. The default-bundle and retire controls move to the end.

1cA list, and a page for each bundlePicked · owner, 9 Oct

Why I recommend it. People, Projects and Apps already work as a list with a page for each item, and this makes Bundles the same. The bundle gets the full width, and the phone needs nothing special.
