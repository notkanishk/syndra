1 · The first 100ms, in the future tense (P2)

One access row on a person's page, drawn at each moment. At rest, its verbs. In the frame after the press, the seat crossfades (S4) to a forecast in the plan row's tense, amber, and its dot breathes while the request is on its way. On the response the dot changes colour where it sits and the word crossfades to the true one; the bloom plays once, only here, only for something that went well. A failure appears still, and the verbs return beside it. The seat is sized to its taller content, so nothing below moves at any moment.
