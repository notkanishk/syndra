Decisions

1 · The flow

Top to bottom: what the app reads, then the format and the preview side by side, then test people, history, and moving off Actions v1. In the preview, the five checks are a trail that ends in the token: dot and sentence each, check 5 is the token itself. A failed check says its fix under it; a fix in Zitadel says "in Zitadel" and is a violet link. The other checks still run, so the trail points at the one broken step.

2 · A draft previews itself

While the format has unsaved edits, the token shows now and after in one block. A line that leaves starts with − and is struck through; a line that joins starts with + and is bold. A sentence under the block names each difference in words. No colour carries it.

3 · Test people

A card under the format and preview, up to five people. Add a test person opens the person form: search, then one ChoiceRow per person, with what they hold in this project in the note seat before you pick. While editing, the table shows Now, After this draft and Difference.

4 · Saving: both

The before-line under Save always says the reach ("reaches 3 apps and 41 people"). Save opens a dialog that lists each difference, each app's match, and a note for History. It ends on Saved, in the sheet.

5 · History is a card

At the foot of the page, newest first. A version is the whole project's format, because one token carries all of it. Go back to this version opens a dialog that shows the difference, then saves the old version again as a new one.

6 · What the app reads

The first card on the page: one row per claim the app reads, with Matches or Doesn't match (dot and word). Its one write place. The apps index shows each app's own claim name, and the verdict in place of the mixed-format guess.

7 · Moving off Actions v1

On the app page, the last card: a checklist and a pasted v1 token compared line by line. Drawn as advice. Owner's call: whether it gates anything (my pick: it doesn't), and whether Syndra keeps the pasted token (my pick: it doesn't).

8 · Static values have a type

Choosing "the same value for everyone…" adds a Segmented: Text, Number, Yes/no, List. Each row ends with "Sent as" and the exact value in mono, so the type shows as the token will carry it. A saved value keeps its type; nothing is guessed.

Options

Where there was more than one good answer. The frames below use my pick.

1 · Where the checks sit
1aThe trail ends in the tokenMy pick

Reads in order, cause to effect. Check 5 is the token, so an empty token sits right under the step that emptied it, with the fix.

1bA strip above the token

Five sentences don't fit a strip. Each check shrinks to a word, and the fix moves into a hover nobody finds.

1cFolded under the token
⌄ Show the five checks

Calm when everything passes, but an empty token sits there without its reason, and the reason is what people open this page for.

5 · Where history lives
5aA card at the footMy pick

Always there, one scroll from the editor, so "what was it before?" never needs a click. Going back is a row action with its own dialog.

5bA tab

Splits one page in two. The preview and the checks go out of sight just when you want to compare a version with them.

5cA sheet

Going back would be a pushed step inside it, which works, but reading the list costs a dialog every time.

7 · Does the v1 checklist gate anything? Owner's call
7aAdvice onlyMy pick

The v1 scripts live in Zitadel; Syndra can't see them or turn them off. A gate in Syndra would guard nothing. The card says what is true and the owner decides when to switch.

7bA gate on Syndra's claims

Syndra adds no claims to an app's tokens until its checklist passes. Safer during the move, but it is a second switch per app, and an app that never had v1 waits behind a list that doesn't apply to it.
