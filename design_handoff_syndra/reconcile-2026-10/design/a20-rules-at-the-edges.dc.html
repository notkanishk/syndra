Rules at the edges

A-20, part one of three. Three rules already settled and not yet applied where they matter most: the shell's own Notice sits above every page's header (G7); sixteen lists keep their column headings over an empty state (G9); and names still do not link (G14), which is why the name does not always arrive with you. Nothing new moves. The product gets the rules it already has.

What you asked

Go over everything again. Rich, fluid, organic, flowing, elegant; the app as an extension of the user.

What I recommend here

The Notice under the header: PageHeader owns a slot for the shell's offline and degraded Notices, so the title is the first thing on every page, on every outage. Headings leave with the rows: ListStates draws the column headings only when there are rows under them. Names link: UserName, ProjectName and BundleName link by default; only a page's own subject does not.

1 · The shell's Notice, under the header (P3)

People, while Zitadel is not answering. Today the degraded Notice is the first thing in the content column, above the title, on every page (AppShell.tsx:94-97). Proposed, PageHeader keeps a slot 16 below its lede, and the shell's Notices render there. The amber dot breathes as it does today; the Notice still opens by Unfold. The title is the first thing on the page, which is what the rule was for.

2 · Headings leave with the rows (P4)

People, with a search that matches nobody. Today the column headings stay over the empty state on sixteen lists (users/page.tsx:399 and 426 among them). Proposed, ListStates takes the headings as its head and draws them only with rows. The empty state is a sentence with a full stop and one outline sm button, as G9 says.

3 · Names link (P1)

A Requests row and a Pending changes row. Today the names are plain text on every queue (components/names/ has no link; only two local copies on Home and Pending link). Proposed, UserName, ProjectName and BundleName link by default: ink at rest, accent with the underline tinting in on hover (A-08 2a). One link per destination per row, so the role beside the person links its project only when the project is not already named. The second row of each pair shows the hover.
