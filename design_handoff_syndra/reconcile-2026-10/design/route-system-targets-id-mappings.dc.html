Mappings

Which roles reach a connected system, and the published versions of that set. Reached from the system's page; no rail row. One band holds the top permanently and always says one of three things: the rows match the newest version, the rows are ahead of it, or nothing is published yet. Each frame is the real shell and components; open one alone with ?frame= and its name.

Phase 2 · area 1

#13 drawn, owner's ruling applied: a mapping is a role and a group. The role comes from the system's one Zitadel project, the group from the add-on's own list, so no project field, no typed setting and no typed value. The setting a mapping fills comes from the add-on's schema; with one, it is the field's label. Implementation: proposed/Mapping-implementation-notes.md.
