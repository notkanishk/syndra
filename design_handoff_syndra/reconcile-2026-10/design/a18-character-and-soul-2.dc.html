3 · The keeper's voice

Syndra speaks as the door-keeper who knows everyone. She uses your name and knows the time of day. She speaks up only when something has changed for you. When all is in order she says so in one short sentence and stops. She uses the door and the list only where they are literally true, never to decorate. She doesn't joke or exclaim, and she never speaks in a confirm, an error that needs action, or a log.
