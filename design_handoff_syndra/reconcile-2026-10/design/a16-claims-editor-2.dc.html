What an app is told, simply

A-16, revising A-15's shape card and A-14's claim rows. The card is now one short list: each claim the app gets, said in words, with the roles claim first. That is the 90% job, and it is visible on arrival. Everything else opens in place, from the line it belongs to. Behaviour, API and the owner's rulings stand.

What you saw on 4 Nov

A summary, then the roles table, always open, at about 58px a row. Then a fold of five full forms that said the summary again. Bare-text buttons beside outlined ones, 46px fields, a segmented switch on every roles row, and Add a claim as a blank form.

What it becomes

One list, where the summary is the editor. The roles claim comes first, the rest A to Z. Each line opens in place with the grouped list's chevron. The roles table is folded and its line says what changed. Add a claim is a short menu of finished claims. A button on its own is outline sm, and fields are the sm control's height.

Decisions

1 · The shape card

One list of claims. Each line is the key in mono, what it carries in words, and a faint line where one helps. The roles claim is the hero because it is first, not because it is bigger: groups, "The roles each person holds in Network, as a list", then "All 7 roles, in Syndra's words." The other claims follow A to Z. Lands in and the template move to the card's foot. The Claim rows fold is gone, because every line now opens its own editor.

2 · Roles, folded

The roles line opens the roles. It is closed on every arrival, and its faint line says the state: "All 7 roles, in Syndra's words." or "6 of 7 roles. Staff as netbird-staff; Guests left out." Opened, it shows the claim's name and Sent as (Advanced). Then comes the roles table at about 44px a row, with name and key on one line, a quiet word field and Included. Roles added later (Advanced) closes it.

3 · Adding a claim

Recommended (a). Add a claim opens a short menu of finished claims, named the way apps expect. Picking one adds a line that already works. Two need one choice, which roles or what value, and open straight into their editor. The keys Zitadel already sends are listed greyed, saying so. Nesting is "Inside", a field in a claim's editor. Text formats are Sent as, in the roles line's editor.

4 · Controls

A button on its own is outline sm, and ghost appears only as the quieter half of a pair (Discard the draft, beside Save). Remove is outline danger sm. Fields are 34px with a mouse and 44 on touch. A segmented control appears only inside an opened line. Opening anything in place uses the 28px round chevron, and the whole line is the control. The full list is below.

5 · Basic

The same list, with nothing to change on it except the roles. The roles line still opens, showing only the words and Included. There is no Add a claim, no claim editor and no Lands in control, and the foot line says where the claims land.

6 · Motion

Every line opens by Unfold, and one stays open until you close it. A claim from the menu arrives as a FormPart: its gap opens in its A-to-Z place and it tints in. Nothing travels. The menu is an AnchoredPanel, popover-in, and closes with tint-out. Save unfolds with your first change, as in A-15.
