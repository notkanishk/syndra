Insights: the map, and how access is changing

One nav item, two tabs. Map opens first and answers questions about how access is shaped today, or on any past date. Trends covers what a map cannot draw: how long things take, what is ending, what is held but never used, and how delivery has behaved. Every number on Trends opens the map or a filtered list in the matching state. The map is one click from the sidebar, as it is now. Every frame is live; open one alone with ?frame=.

The frames still mark the old nav item, because the label lives in the nav itself; the rename is on the build list.

Four places on the Map tab, and nothing else

The question, in the search seat. One field. Type a name and it offers sentences: Follow, Path from … to …, Compare … with …, What if …, Who has … without …. A plain question becomes one of those sentences, and every part of the sentence can be edited.

Lens, in the pill seat. People · Recent use · Ending soon · A group. Only one lens at a time, and the lens decides what a line's weight means. Only the colours already in use appear.

The read line. Left: how current the map is, and the way back in time. Right: findings, shown only when there are any. A finding chip lights its spots; it is not a setting.

The panel. Answers the current question in sentences, and links to the page where the change is made. Its footer keeps up to three pins and copies the answer as sentences.

No chrome at all: the link always holds the current state; the keys sit behind ?; the TrueNAS lane folds out of its own node; drift and waiting-to-send are marks on the lines at all times, because they are facts, not modes.

Where it lives: A, B, C

B is drawn above. A and C, small, for comparison.

A · The map is the page Simplest. Trends go to Home as stat cards that open the map. Nowhere to see how long things take or what is ending by space.
B · Insights: Map, then Trends · chosen The map stays one click away and opens first. Trends holds change over time, and every number leads back to the map or a list.
C · Home carries everything Crowds Home, whose job is what needs you today. The map shrinks to a tile.
Keys

/ asks. ← → move along a line and follow as they go; ↑ ↓ move within a lane; P pins; T opens the date; L cycles the lens; ? shows the keys; Esc steps back one question. Keyboard moves get the same draw-in as a click.

Colour, kept to its meaning

Violet is what you are asking about, in up to three shades when threads are pinned or compared. Amber is a deadline or a broken assumption: waiting for you to send, a single route, a removal preview. Red is drift only. Lenses never add colour; they change weight and fade.

Left out

3D or free-floating layouts, which give up direction for spectacle. Editing by dragging lines, a second unreviewed way to change access. A "riskiest" ranking, a health score or leaderboards of people: Syndra can count, not guess. Nothing on Insights writes; every change happens on the thing's own page.
