The first visit, the phone, and the first 100ms

A-20, part two of three. Four things that are nearly right: the welcome adds a row on Home and hides the ladder on My access (P2); a phone in Basic has no way to Go to, and no mark to light once (P6); an error names the thing that failed and stops (P7); the two acts with the highest stakes, Drift and Remove, are the two with no forecast (P8).

What you asked

Go over everything again. The app as an extension of the user, on every device and in the first frame of every act.

What I recommend here

The welcome keeps its seat: it is the title's second sentence, and the line below still says the one thing worth knowing. The phone's top bar gains the mark at left and Go to at right; the four tabs stay four. Errors finish the sentence: which failed, which did not, what is true, what to do, and the reference. The forecast everywhere: Drift, Remove, Give, bulk and Cancel say what Syndra will do in the first frame.

1 · The welcome keeps its seat (P2)

My access on Priya's first visit, the day her welcome roles were given. Today the welcome takes the ladder's line (MemberAccess.tsx:226), so she is not told she can use Laser cutting from today. Proposed, "Welcome to Syndra." is the title's second sentence, in the seat "Here's what you can use." holds on every later visit, and the line below keeps the ladder. When nothing is on the ladder, its calm rung reads the About sentence that day. Nothing moves between the first visit and the second.

Home, on the operator's first visit, when the day is calm: the greeting's second sentence is the welcome, and the calm panel carries the About sentence once. On a working day the headline wins and the welcome is not said: the work is the welcome.

2 · The phone's top bar (P6, P15)

Basic, on a phone. The bar gains the SyndraMark at its left, the same drawing as the rail's, so the one-shot bloom after sign-in has a seat on a phone; and a Go to button at its right, beside the avatar, for operators in both views. It opens the sheet the Advanced nav sheet already holds, Input at the top. The four tabs stay four: Go to is not a destination, so it is not a tab. Members get neither: three pages need no jump. Drawn inline; the bar is the TopBar's own change.

3 · An error that finishes the sentence (P7)

People, failing to load. Today ErrorState names the thing and says "Nothing was changed.", then stops; a network fault passes the browser's own words through. Proposed, the four parts the copy guide asks for: which thing failed and which did not, the state of the world, the next step and where, and the reference with "Quote this if you ask for help." The page keeps its title and lede on error, as the rule says.

4 · The forecast on Drift (P8)

One Drift row in four moments: at rest, pressed and busy, forecasting, and the true word. The forecast is Syndra's own next step in the plan row's tense: "Will be revoked", amber dot, no link. Revocations send on their own, so the true word is "On its way" with a neutral dot, as A-19 P14 settled; it does not bloom, because nothing has landed yet. The same four moments reach Remove, Give a role, the bulk dialogs and Cancel a queued change. A role that waits forecasts "Will wait for you to send", never "Will be approved".
