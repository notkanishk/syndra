Choice 6 · when something is wrong

Same anatomy for every dysfunction: tinted box, dot, a title in its tone, one ink sentence, the way out as an outline button. Choice 9 · vertical rhythm

One set of distances on every page

Today each page picks its own stack gap: 28 on Home, 22 on People, a person and an app, 18 on Roles, Projects, Apps and Bundles. Everything in a page sits that one distance apart, so a filter bar is as far from its title as a card is from the card below it, and nothing reads as belonging together. Roles is the worst case: title, filter bar, freshness line, a sentence and the list are five strata at 18 · 18 · 10 · 18, and the gap under the lede looks the same as every other gap. Freshness also moves: above the list on Roles, Projects and Apps; under it on People; in the header on a project. Drawn at real desktop size (1160px of content, as at 1440 with the rail) from the top of Roles; the violet rulers are the distances.
