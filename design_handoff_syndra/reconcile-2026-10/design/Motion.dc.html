Motion

Every movement in Syndra is one of six, and each moment below is built from them. Route boards point here instead of describing motion again. Each demo loops; the line under it names the step on screen. Where two good answers exist they sit side by side — pick one by its id.

With reduced motion on, every movement has a still form: the new state appears in place, nothing travels, and the breathing dot rests at full strength. Use the Tweaks panel to slow the demos or see the still forms.
