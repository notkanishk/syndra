Rule-derived changes in a plan

A-21, Q3 (planner decision 7). A role an automatic rule adds is a sentence inside the person's row, never a row of its own: "Also gives Operator in Laser Lab, by the automatic rule from Trained in Laser Lab." Its delivery counts like any other write when the person's fold is chosen, so a rule-given role that waits puts the person under the amber fold and is named in the waits sentence like any other. The same sentence is what Give and Approve say before the press, from the same planner.
